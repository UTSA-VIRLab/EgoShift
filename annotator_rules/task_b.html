How to decide
  1. You are the camera. You look straight at the middle of the picture. The object may be anywhere within your view.
  2. Turn your body by the amount the question gives. The room and the object do not move. Only you turn.
  3. Where is the object now, relative to the way you are now facing?
__MAP__ Every question is at least 15° clear of a zone boundary, so none of them is a borderline call.
