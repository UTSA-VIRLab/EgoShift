How to decide

View 1 boxes the object. Look at View 2 and ask, in order:

  1. Can you see any part of it? If yes — even a small part — it is Visible.
  2. If you cannot see it: is View 2 still showing the part of the room where the object was? Use the furniture and walls around the box in View 1 as landmarks.
    • That part of the room is not in View 2 — the camera has turned or moved away from it → Out of View.
    • That part of the room is in View 2, but something bulky now stands where the object was → Occluded.
Out of View and Occluded both mean you cannot see it; the difference is where the camera points. Ask "is the object's spot on screen?" — spot on screen but object hidden: Occluded; spot itself off screen: Out of View.
